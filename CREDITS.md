# Credits

## Textures (`textures/`)
From [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Based on NASA imagery.

- Sun, Mercury, Venus (atmosphere), Earth (day, night lights, clouds), Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn's rings, Uranus, Neptune
- Milky Way star map
- Ceres, Eris, Haumea, Makemake: these are **artist's impressions** ("fictional" maps), since no full real maps exist

Surfaces for the other moons (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Rhea, Titan, Titania, Oberon, Triton, Charon),
Pluto and the comet nuclei are generated in code (`js/textures.js`).

## Library
[three.js](https://threejs.org) r149 (MIT), loaded from jsDelivr.

## Data
- Masses and radii: NASA Planetary and Satellite Fact Sheets
- Planet and Pluto orbits: JPL "Approximate Positions of the Planets" (J2000 elements)
- Dwarf planet and comet orbits: JPL Small-Body Database
